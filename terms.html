  • Grammar, tense, voice, narration, transformation and vocabulary lessons, exam vocabulary (synonyms and antonyms, one-word substitution, prepositions, phrasal verbs, collocations, spelling), a Spoken English course (basic to advanced), Writing Skills and Translation lessons, and a board-exam preparation section (SSC and HSC English 2nd Paper model tests, narration practice, model paragraphs and compositions) — all with Bengali explanations and examples, built into the app.
