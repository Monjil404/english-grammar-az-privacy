1. Information you give us

  • None. The app has no account, login, forms or chat. You are never asked to type personal information.
  • If you contact us yourself (email or Facebook), we receive only what you choose to send and use it only to reply to you.

2. Information stored only on your device

  • Your study data: which lessons you have read and how far, the lesson you read last (“continue reading”), bookmarks, best quiz scores, exam results, flashcard progress and your daily study streak.
  • Settings: theme (light, dark or follow the phone), reading text size, keeping the screen on while reading, exam preferences and whether the daily word reminder is on.
  • Small counters used to limit how often ads appear and to decide when to politely ask for a Play Store rating.
  • This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage) deletes all of it.
  • If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include this study data in your own encrypted Google backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is controlled by you and Google; we cannot see it.

4. Lessons, pronunciation and app updates

  • All lessons, quizzes and flashcards are stored inside the app and are shown with Android System WebView. Lessons do not load anything from the internet.
  • When you tap an English example or a flashcard's “play” button, the text is passed to the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone (for example, Speech Services by Google). We do not receive or store it. The engine's own privacy policy applies.
উদাহরণের উচ্চারণ শোনার সময় লেখাটি আপনার ফোনের text-to-speech ইঞ্জিনে পাঠানো হয়; আমরা তা পাই না বা সংরক্ষণ করি না।

5. Advertising and your choices

  • The app is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob (including ads from Meta Audience Network that AdMob selects through mediation). Ads are shown at limited, natural moments with minimum gaps between them, and never on the first launch.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions where it is required, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. In those regions you can change your choice anytime in the app: Settings → Manage Ad Preferences (also in About).
  • Ads are rated for general audiences (maximum ad content rating “G”).
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).

6. Notifications

  • The optional “আজকের শব্দ” (word of the day) reminder is off until you turn it on. It is created on your phone from words already inside the app — no server sends it.
  • On Android 13 and later you are asked for notification permission only when you turn it on. You can turn it off anytime in About or in your phone's settings.
